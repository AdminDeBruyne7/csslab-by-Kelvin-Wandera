About Me

I'm a first-year student in the evening Bachelor of Business Information Technology program at Strathmore University in Nairobi, having previously completed a Diploma in Business Information Technology at the same institution. Classes run in the evening, which leaves my days free for independent projects — right now that means building a full website for my father's construction company using Laravel and React.

Outside of coding, I shoot and edit video under my own content brand, working primarily in Adobe Premiere Pro. I like treating a video project the same way I treat a codebase: plan the structure first, then execute with attention to detail. I also enjoy working with data — there's a similar satisfaction in finding a clean pattern in a spreadsheet as there is in fixing a stubborn bug.
